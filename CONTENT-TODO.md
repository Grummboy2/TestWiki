# Content still worth adding

The guide only states things that were in the original wiki. Where something was not documented, the page says so
("Not covered yet") instead of guessing. These gaps are the biggest wins for making this the best guide available:

## High value
- **How to get a dragon egg in Update 2.0 Drop 1** (nests, crafting, drops?). The home page "first hour" has a placeholder-level step for this.
- **Taming and feeding in 2.0.** What food tames, heals and breeds dragons? The older v1.2.x notes say any meat except pufferfish to tame and any raw fish except pufferfish to breed. Confirm in-game, then replace the "Older versions" notes in `breeding.html`.
- **Crafting recipes** for every item and armor set (add a recipe grid or ingredient list to each item card in `items.html`).
- **How to bind dragons** for V-Formation Flight. The wiki says "up to 4" but not how.
- **Light Dragon details:** rideable? Can it breed? Takeoff speed? Where do its scales come from?

## Nice to have
- Screenshots of each dragon, each growth stage and each egg (save as `.webp` in `assets/img/`).
- What the Dragon Scepter, Flutes, Core and Netherite Shears actually do.
- Armor stats and set bonuses.
- Dragon Keeper: where it appears and what it does.
- Known bugs and workarounds, per version.
- Controls table for mobile, console and keyboard.
