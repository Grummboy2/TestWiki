#Your first hour

The shortest route from download to flying.

  1. Install and enable both packsTurn on the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack in your world.
  2. Get a dragon eggEggs were redesigned in Update 2.0 Drop 1. Fire and Ice eggs come from breeding, and the starting parent decides which.
  3. Raise itEvery dragon grows from Baby to Juvenile to Adult. Dragon collars are available too.
  4. Ride itFire and Ice dragons are rideable.
  5. Take offDouble jump while riding to start Dynamic Flight.

#New in Update 2.0 Drop 1

  • Backend rewrite and a new add-on ID, dragonmounts2.
  • Light Dragon, the third dragon, with its own scales and armor.
  • Dynamic Flight: double jump to take off.
  • V-Formation Flight: bind up to 4 dragons.
  • Armor overhaul, including Leather, Netherite, Light Feather and two bone sets.
